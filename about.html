About

Hi, I'm Bhanu.

SCAD Honors Award Best VFX — Indian Telly Streaming Awards 2024 MFA, ITGM — SCAD, 2027 Shoorveer — Disney+ Hotstar

I've always been into video games and movies. Growing up it was just a hobby, but once I finished my undergrad in Computer Science, Mathematics, and Electronics, I figured I had enough of a foundation to actually go after it. That's when I found virtual production — using game engines to build and shoot cinematic scenes in real time. It let me do both at once.

I got pretty obsessed with it, and one thing led to another until I landed a role on a television show. Switching fields hasn't been easy, but it's been exciting. I'm currently pursuing an MFA in Interactive Media and Game Development at SCAD (expected June 2027), coming at that same overlap from the design side this time — interactive design, systems, and player intent.

My undergrad in Computer Science, Mathematics, and Electronics still shapes how I get there. I default to breaking a problem into its parts and testing each one before committing to a final approach — which is most of why the research side of my MFA has stuck with me as much as the production side. It's the same instinct either way: don't just make something work, understand why it works.

What I actually do

I'm an Unreal Engine artist and virtual production generalist. In practice that means: hard-surface modeling in Maya, texturing in Substance Painter, lighting and set dressing in Unreal Engine, and cinematography — whether that's a camera move in Sequencer or a real production shoot on an LED volume.

I worked as an Unreal Engine Engineer on Disney+ Hotstar's Shoorveer, India's first television show produced in Unreal Engine and one of the first anywhere to use LED volumes for cockpit sequences — it went on to win Best VFX (Show, Hindi) at the Indian Telly Streaming Awards 2024. Outside of that I keep a running series of personal environment studies, and I've modeled everything from an F-22 Raptor to the Space Shuttle to my own family's house.
