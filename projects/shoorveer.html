Disney+ Hotstar — Shoorveer

Unreal Engine cinematography and virtual production on India's first television show produced in a real-time engine.

Unreal Engine 5LED VolumeCesiumCinematographyUltra Dynamic Sky

I worked as an Unreal Engine Generalist on Shoorveer — Digital Cinematography, with Viga Entertainment, 2021–22 — one of the few television productions built entirely on Unreal Engine, and my first professional project on the engine.

My work centered on the show's Air Combat Maneuver (ACM) shots: keeping flight physics believable and tweaking camera work shot by shot. Tasks included cinematography, landscape setup using Cesium, and basic lighting and cloud coverage for aerial sequences.

Shoorveer was India's first television show made in Unreal Engine, and one of the first anywhere to use LED volumes for cockpit sequences. I helped set up environments and worked on real-time lighting inside those volumes.

All shots shown were done in real time in Unreal Engine — camera placement, animation feedback for the animation team, and fighter jet motion were all given equal weight in the process, across multiple episodes.
