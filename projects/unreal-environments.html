Unreal Environments

A running series of personal lighting studies — each one an excuse to chase a specific mood, material, or camera move.

Unreal Engine 5.1 / 5.2LumenRay TracingQuixel BridgeSequencer

Mostly personal work built over the past few years, each one testing a different lighting problem, plus one professional piece:

Baby Driver car animation — Project Lead, RayTrace Studio, 2023. A drivable car sequence rigged and animated to match a given cinematic reference, with a full lighting setup built for photorealism. I oversaw the piece end to end and delivered the final scene to client expectations.

Mughal architecture dreamscape — a study in blending complex lighting across a large number of objects, built on Engine 5.1 and later updated to 5.2, using Epic's asset library. The goal was depicting old Mughal architecture somewhere it would never actually exist.

Abandoned apartment / horror piece — one of my first cinematic Unreal Engine projects, built from the Abandoned Apartment asset pack and set-dressed with horror elements. Lighting was refined afterward in Premiere Pro.

Game teaser — a short teaser built from Epic Store assets, going for a dark, wet environment to practice complex lighting and sharpen camera/sequencer skills.

Dark environment, work in progress — a self-designed environment testing lighting under dark conditions with layered particle systems (smoke, steam, snow), using Quixel Bridge assets alongside custom materials aimed at realism.

B-2 Spirit animation — a short animation and lighting pass on a B-2 Spirit bomber.
