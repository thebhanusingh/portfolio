ArchViz — Ancestral Home

A remake of my family's ancestral home, based on the architect's plan for its renovation — rebuilt so it could stand again, digitally.

BlenderUnreal EnginePersonal

This project remakes my ancestral home based on the plan produced by the architect handling its renovation. I added interior elements on top of the base plan to make the project feel lived-in rather than just structural.

The house was originally built in the early 1930s, so the renovation called for an updated floor plan. Once I'd redone the floor map, I brought it into Blender as a base for the 3D model. The first task was calibration — scaling the reference image so its printed scale matched Blender's units exactly. With that locked in, I rebuilt the house in 3D and imported the model into Unreal Engine for lighting and set dressing.
